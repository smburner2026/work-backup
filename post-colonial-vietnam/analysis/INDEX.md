## Synthesis: What Phạm Văn Sơn Is Building

Across all four lenses, a consistent picture emerges:

| Lens | What He Sees | What He Misses |
|------|--------------|----------------|
| **Burckhardt** | Elite Confucian culture under threat | Religious diversity, ethnic fragmentation, regional differences, women, popular religion |
| **Nietzschean Vitalism** | Individual force and will (Thuyết, Phùng, Tráng) | Population biology, ecology, disease environment, demographic substrate, reproduction |
| **Class** | National unity against France | Land relations, class conflict, material interests, the exploitation within Vietnamese society |
| **Covert+Luttwak** | Battles and blunders | Grand strategy, covert apparatus, systemic logic, second-order effects |

**The core finding:** Phạm Văn Sơn is constructing a *national myth*, not a historical analysis. His "Vietnam" is culturally monolithic, great-man-driven, class-unified, and heroically tragic. This is what post-colonial historians in the Third World普遍 do — construct a usable past. The four lenses reveal exactly *what* he suppresses and *why* — and point to the primary sources that would fill the gaps.

## Source-Finding Strategy (Aggregated)

### For Burckhardt (cultural dimension)
- EFEO ethnographic studies
- Catholic mission records (Aix-en-Provence)
- Mường oral traditions
- Chinese-language sources (Qing court records)
- Vietnamese popular religion studies

### For Vitalism (biological dimension)
- French colonial census data (1880s-1890s)
- Ecological and disease studies
- Archaeological evidence (Tân Sở, Ba Đình, Hương Khê)
- Population genetics studies
- Colonial-era medical reports

### For Class (material dimension)
- French colonial economic reports
- Nguyễn dynasty land and tax records (Huế archives, EFEO microfilms)
- Catholic mission records on community structure
- Plantation records (rubber, rice)
- Mường economic relations with lowland Vietnamese

### For Covert+Luttwak (strategic dimension)
- French diplomatic archives (Quai d'Orsay, Nantes)
- French military intelligence (Vincennes)
- British diplomatic records (Kew)
- Chinese diplomatic archives
- Missionary intelligence networks
- Captured resistance documents
