# Glossary — Chương VI: Pháp Đuổi Theo Vua Hàm-Nghi
## (The French Pursue King Ham Nghi)

### Key Historical Figures

| Term | Meaning / Notes |
|------|-----------------|
| **Vua Hàm-Nghi** (1871–1944) | Eighth emperor of the Nguyễn dynasty (r. 1884–1885). Issued the Cần-Vương (Aid the King) edict calling for resistance against French colonial rule. Captured in 1888 and exiled to Algiers. |
| **Tôn-thất-Thuyết** | Regent (Phụ chính đại thần) who led the royalist resistance after the 1885 Huế coup. Escaped to China after the fall of the Cần-Vương movement. |
| **Tôn-thất-Thiệp** | Second son of Tôn-thất-Thuyết, served as Ham Nghi's close bodyguard. Killed defending the king during the betrayal. |
| **Tôn-thất-Đạm** | Senior official (Khâm-sai) of the Cần-Vương movement. Committed suicide after Ham Nghi's capture. |
| **Trương-quang-Ngọc** | Mường chieftain (thổ-ty) from Thanh-Lạng. Initially served as the king's protector and military commander. Later betrayed Ham Nghi to the French for reward. |
| **Trần-xuân-Soạn** | Đề-đốc (Admiral/General) who accompanied the royal entourage. Later fled to China with Tôn-thất-Thuyết. |
| **Lê-Trực** (Đề-đốc Lê-Trực) | Prominent Cần-Vương commander in Quảng-Bình. Held out against French forces for years. |
| **Nguyễn-phạm-Tuân** (Tri-sự) | Cần-Vương commander based at Yên-Lương. Captured and killed by Mouteaux. |
| **Nguyễn-đình-Tình** | Royal guard (suất-đội) who defected to the French and helped orchestrate the king's capture. |
| **Phạm-văn-Mỹ** | Collaborator who advised the French on how to corrupt Trương-quang-Ngọc. |
| **Cao-Viết-Lượng** | Local from Thanh-Cước who fatally stabbed Tôn-thất-Thiệp. |
| **Phan-đình-Bình** | Thượng-thư (Minister) sent by the Nguyễn court to pacify Quảng-Bình. |
| **Võ-bá-Liêm** | Official sent on pacification mission; captured and executed by the resistance. |
| **Đồng-Khánh** (r. 1885–1889) | Nguyễn emperor installed by the French to replace Hàm-Nghi. |
| **Tự-đức** (r. 1847–1883) | Penultimate independent Nguyễn emperor; father figure to the earlier generation. |
| **Đinh-Hiền, Đinh-Đối, Đinh-Trọng** | Local hosts who sheltered the king at Qui-Đạt. |

### French Military & Colonial Figures

| Term | Meaning / Notes |
|------|-----------------|
| **Hugo** | Đại-úy (Captain), French commander wounded by Ngọc at Núi Lập-Cập; died of poison. |
| **Camus** | Trung-úy (Lieutenant), killed at sông Vé in January 1886. |
| **Freystatter** | Trung-úy, Camus's second-in-command; withdrew after Camus's death. |
| **Pelletier** | Thiếu-tá (Major), led French pursuit columns in Quảng-Bình. |
| **Plagnol** | Thiếu-tá, co-commander of French offensive at Cửa Khe. |
| **Metzinger** | Trung-tá (Lieutenant Colonel), commander of French forces in Quảng-Bình. |
| **Mignot** | Trung-tá, co-commander. |
| **Mouteaux** | Đại-úy, aggressive French commander who conducted savage campaigns. |
| **Boulangier** | Đại-úy, corresponded with Ngọc to arrange the king's betrayal. |
| **Prudhomme** | Thiếu-tướng (Major General), directed the Ham Nghi pursuit; later dismissed. |
| **Warnet** | Trung-tướng (Lieutenant General), criticized Prudhomme's approach. |
| **Paul-Bert** | French civilian Governor-General (arrived April 1886); shifted policy. |
| **de Courcy** | French general who led the 1885 Huế attack. |
| **Rheinart** | French official who interviewed Hàm-Nghi after capture. |
| **Bonnefoy** | Trung-úy, escorted the king to exile. |
| **Dabat** | Thiếu-tá, received the king at Thuận-Bài fort. |
| **Lagarrue** | Trung-úy, received Ngọc's secret surrender in October 1888. |
| **Ch. Gosselin** | French historian/officer who chronicled the campaigns. |

### Dates & Chronology

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1885 (Ất-dậu)** | King Ham Nghi flees Huế; Cần-Vương edict issued. |
| **November 1885** | French forces begin major pursuit operations. |
| **January 1886** | Camus killed at sông Vé; Ngọc's Mường forces win three consecutive battles. |
| **Late January 1886** | Battle at Trại Na; Tôn-thất-Thuyết decides to seek Chinese aid. |
| **February 1886** | French intensify search for the king. |
| **April 8, 1886** | Paul-Bert arrives in Hanoi; new policy era begins. |
| **May 14, 1886** | Native auxiliary battalions disbanded and converted to light infantry. |
| **Mid-1887** | Mouteaux's brutal campaigns in Quảng-Bình. |
| **October 12, 1888** | Trương-quang-Ngọc secretly surrenders to Lagarrue. |
| **November 3, 1888** | King Hàm-Nghi captured at Khe Ta-Ban. |
| **December 15, 1888** | King transported from Bố-trạch to Đồng-Hới. |
| **December 25, 1888** | King departs from Lăng-Cô aboard La Comète. |
| **January 13, 1889** (Sunday) | King arrives in Algiers aboard the Biên-Hòa. |
