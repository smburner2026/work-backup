# Post-Colonial Vietnam — Source Request

## About This Project

I'm researching Vietnam from pre-World War II through the early 1960s — from the Nguyen dynasty and French colonial period, through the resistance, Japanese occupation, First Indochina War, and the rise and fall of Diem. The goal is to understand this period through multiple lenses: culture, biography, power dynamics, class, and the covert operations that shaped events.

I'd like your help identifying sources, people, and materials to explore.

---

## What I'm Looking For

### 1. Key People

Who were the important figures in each period? Not just the famous ones — minor figures, local leaders, intellectuals, military officers, religious leaders, businesspeople, anyone who shaped or represented their era.

For each person, I'd like to know:
- Their name (in Vietnamese and English if possible)
- What period they belong to
- What they did / why they matter
- Any books or articles written about them

### 2. Biographies and Memoirs

Any book about a specific person's life from this period. Both:
- **Western-language biographies** (English, French)
- **Vietnamese-language biographies** (official and unofficial)

Also: autobiographies, diaries, memoirs, letters — firsthand accounts from anyone who lived through this period.

### 3. Primary Sources

Original documents from the period:
- Government records (French colonial, Vietnamese, American)
- Newspapers and magazines
- Photographs, films, audio recordings
- Personal letters and diaries
- Official histories

If you know where these are held (archives, libraries, personal collections), even better.

### 4. Secondary Sources

Books, articles, or documentaries that analyze this period. Even if they're in Vietnamese and I need translation help, the leads are valuable.

### 5. DNA & Family History

Ancestral DNA test results, family origin stories, old photographs with material culture (buildings, clothing, tools), and oral traditions about migration or village origins. Any physical artifacts from the period — old coins, ceramics, tools, clothing, documents — are also valuable.

## Period Breakdown

Here are the periods I'm covering, with some figures already identified.

### Period 1 — Nguyen Dynasty (1802–1887)
*The imperial order before French conquest.*

**Already looking at:** Gia Long, Minh Mạng, Tự Đức, Hàm Nghi

**Need:** Other Nguyen figures, court intellectuals, poets, regional leaders, Vietnamese-language histories of the dynasty.

### Period 2 — French Colonial Period (1887–1940)
*Colonial rule: rubber plantations, opium monopoly, cultural transformation.*

**Already looking at:** Phan Bội Châu, Phan Châu Trinh, Nguyễn Thái Học, Paul Doumer

**Need:** Colonial administrators, Vietnamese intellectuals (collaborators and resisters), economic records, cultural figures, anyone who lived through this period and wrote about it. Diaries and memoirs from this era are gold.

### Period 3 — Resistance Leaders (early ICP period, ~1930–1945)
*The revolutionary generation emerges.*

**Already looking at:** Hồ Chí Minh, Võ Nguyên Giáp, Trường Chinh

**Need:** Other ICP figures, regional organizers, intellectuals who joined or rejected the revolution, Comintern connections, anyone who participated in the Nghệ Tĩnh Soviets (1930–31).

### Period 4 — Japanese Occupation (March–August 1945)
*The coup against the French, the famine, the August Revolution.*

**Already looking at:** Bảo Đại, the OSS contact with Hồ Chí Minh

**Need:** Firsthand accounts of the 1944–45 famine, Japanese occupation policies, Vietnamese collaborators with Japan, the transfer of power after Japan's surrender.

### Period 5 — First Indochina War (1946–1954)
*The war that ended French rule. Điện Biên Phủ.*

**Already looking at:** Võ Nguyên Giáp (military), Hồ Chí Minh, French commanders (de Lattre, Navarre)

**Need:** Veterans' accounts (both sides), French military figures, civilian experiences, land reform during wartime, Bình Xuyên origins (Bảy Viễn is already a test case).

### Period 6 — Rise and Fall of Ngô Đình Diệm (1954–1963)
*The Catholic mandarin experiment, the Strategic Hamlets, the Buddhist crisis.*

**Already looking at:** Ngô Đình Diệm, Ngô Đình Nhu, Madame Nhu, Thích Quảng Đức

**Need:** Other figures in Diem's government, Catholic vs Buddhist dynamics, CIA connections, military figures in the South, NLF origins, anyone in the strategic hamlet program — on either side.

### Period 7 — Covert Build-Up (1963–1965)
*The secret war before the public war. Hillaire du Berrier's territory.*

**Already looking at:** Hillaire du Berrier, Colonel Ed Lansdale, Montagnard leaders

**Need:** CIA figures, Special Forces veterans, Montagnard/CIDG participants, Air America people, anyone involved in the early covert operations in Laos and Vietnam.
