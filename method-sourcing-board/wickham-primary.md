## Method signals from verified sources

1. **Comparative method**: 10 regions treated identically, same questions asked of each
2. **Primary sources first**: archaeology + legal documents + letters, before modern interpretations
3. **Social/economic focus**: explicitly not political narrative, not intellectual culture
4. **Annales tradition**: acknowledged but transcended into systematic comparison
5. **Material base**: "how the logic(s) of pre-capitalist economic systems operated on the ground"
6. **Standardized vocabulary**: terms treated as Weberian ideal types
